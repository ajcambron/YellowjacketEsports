# Meeting Procedures & Norms

Meetings run **2:30 to 4:30**. Matches start at **3:30**. All three teams share one match console, so matches run back to back. The activity bus does not wait, so the day is built backward from it.

## Meeting day timeline

| Time | What happens | Player job |
|------|--------------|------------|
| 2:30 | Arrive, sign in | Sign the attendance sheet. Phone away unless the coach says otherwise. |
| 2:35 | Station setup | Log in to your station, plug in your headset, check mic and controls. Report problems now, not at 3:29. |
| 2:45 | Team huddle | Coach posts lineups, subs, and goals for the day. |
| 2:50 | Warm-up / scrims | Practice with your team. Stay on the match title. |
| **3:15** | **Ready up in Fenworks** | **Every rostered player checks in, including subs and anyone not playing the first game.** |
| 3:30 | 1st team plays | On deck: 2nd team sits behind the console with controllers ready. |
| ~3:47 | 2nd team plays | 1st team reports the score and packs up. 3rd team moves on deck. |
| ~4:04 | 3rd team plays | 2nd team reports the score and packs up. |
| **4:20** | **Pack up** | 3rd team and anyone left logs out, shuts down, and puts equipment back. |
| 4:25 | Leave for the bus | Out the door as a group. |

!!! warning "Ready up in Fenworks as soon as you can"
    Check in to the match in Fenworks as soon as you're logged in, even if you aren't playing the first game. If the roster isn't checked in when the match opens, the team can take a forfeit. Subs check in too, so the coach can swap players without losing time.

## Console rotation

Three teams at about 15 minutes each, plus two changeovers, finishes right at 4:20. That leaves almost no slack, so changeovers are where the time is won or lost.

**Order is posted at the huddle.** The coach announces 1st, 2nd, and 3rd. The order rotates week to week so the same team isn't always the one racing the bus.

**On deck means ready.** The next team sits behind the console with controllers plugged in or paired, and their Fenworks lobby open, before the current game ends.

**Hand off the second the game ends.** When the results screen appears, the team that just played stands up and moves away. The captain reports the score from a laptop or phone, not from the console seat. **Target changeover: under 2 minutes.**

**Done playing means pack up.** Teams 1 and 2 run the pack-up checklist as soon as they finish, so at 4:20 only the 3rd team is packing.

**Watch quietly.** Teams not playing can watch, but the playing team gets the space and the quiet.

**Running long.** A Smash match can run up to 35 minutes if every game goes to time, so one long match can push the 3rd team past 4:20. If the 3rd team hasn't started by 4:05, the coach decides what to do before the match begins: start it and arrange rides, or contact the opponent through Fenworks about the delay.

## Pack-up checklist

- [ ] Log out of the game client, Fenworks, Discord, and any personal accounts
- [ ] Shut down or lock the station (follow the coach's call for that day)
- [ ] Headset and controller go back where they came from, cables wrapped
- [ ] Chair pushed in, trash and food wrappers out
- [ ] Grab your bag, phone, and charger
- [ ] Out the door by 4:25

Never leave a live match without telling the coach. A player who stays past 4:20 and misses the bus needs a ride arranged ahead of time.

## Norms

**Show up ready.** If you will miss a match day, tell the coach before 2:30 so the lineup can change. Two unexplained absences can cost your starting spot.

**Ready up first, warm up second.** Fenworks check-in comes before one more practice game.

**Comms are for the game.** Short, specific callouts in voice chat. No yelling at teammates. Save the post-game review for after the game.

**Win and lose the same way.** Say "gg" every time. No trash talk aimed at a person, no slurs, no griefing, no rage quits. The other team is a school too.

**Subs are part of the team.** If you're not playing, watch, take notes for your team, and stay ready to swap in. No playing other games on a sub station during a match.

**Your account is yours.** Never share passwords or play on someone else's account. League rules treat that as cheating.

**Treat the gear like it's borrowed, because it is.** No food or open drinks at the stations. Report broken equipment to the coach. Don't try to fix it yourself.

**School rules still apply.** The school's network use policy, dress code, and discipline policy are in effect during meetings and matches, home or away.

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
|---------|---------|
| Station or game won't launch | Tell the coach right away. Move to the backup station if there is one. |
| Disconnect during a match | Reconnect immediately. Captain messages the opponent and the coach. Don't leave the lobby. |
| Dispute with the other team | Captain screenshots it and gives it to the coach. Players don't argue in chat. |
| Can't find your Fenworks login | See the coach before 3:15, not at 3:30. |
